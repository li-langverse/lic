## Swarm orchestration UX findings

### 1. Operator visibility gap

`data/control-plane/state.json` and `latest-report.json` were absent at audit start. Without these artifacts, the dashboard cannot show retry budgets, `swarm_degraded`, or intervention history. **Impact:** operators cannot self-heal from the UI.

### 2. Gap pipeline friction

| Step | Status | UX impact |
|------|--------|-----------|
| `swarm-gap-ingest.py` | SyntaxError **fixed** this run | unblocks future ticks |
| PyYAML in container | **missing** | ingest/apply fail with opaque exit |
| `runs_sampled=0` in grader | `LI_CURSOR_AGENTS_ROOT` path mismatch | scorecard understates execution health |

### 3. Registry vs snapshot drift

Five `studio-ux-04`…`08` registry gaps remain **open** although snapshot marks them **done**. This creates false-positive gap pressure (score 60.0 on `gap_pressure` dimension) and wastes `gap_explorer` cycles.

### 4. Briefing alignment

Briefing recommends `ci_maintainer`, `security_auditor`, `pr_merger` — aligned with heap_plan P0/P50. No `gui_ux_tester` dispatch despite open `ui_ux_quality` goal; acceptable while control-plane blockers dominate.
